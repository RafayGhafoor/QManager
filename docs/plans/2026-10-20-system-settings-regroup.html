Cards keep their hooks, internals and copy. Only their page changes, plus one control fix so the Hardware pair matches.

Per route

  • Each new page is one cascade root (staggerContainer, initial/animate once). The card grid is a nested container that inherits visible.
  • Cards that swap in after a skeleton declare their own initial="hidden" animate="visible", as the Tracked SIMs row already does.
  • No springs, no loops, no exit animation. Reduced motion goes through useMotionReduced() only.
